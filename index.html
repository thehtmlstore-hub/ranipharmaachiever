How weekly testing works

A simple four-step loop — repeated every week.

Fully online, fully tracked — the same loop every week, so progress never depends on memory or guesswork.

1

Question set released

Every Monday morning, a fresh test paper goes live on the student dashboard — designed to mirror the actual exam pattern for that week's topics.

2

Student attempts

Students attempt the test online within the week, under timed conditions. The format replicates the real exam — same duration, same marking scheme, same pressure.

3

Instant results

Answer keys and evaluated scores are released within 24 hours — visible to the student and the parent. No waiting, no anxiety — you see exactly where you stand.

4

Progress tracked

A personalized feedback report highlights strengths, weak topics, and next-week focus areas — and is shared with parents. Progress is tracked across all 52 weeks of the year.

GPAT subjects covered

The complete GPAT syllabus — subject by subject.

Every subject is taught live by pharmacy specialists and tested in the weekly loop above, so no part of the syllabus is left to chance.

Pharmaceutics

Dosage forms, physical pharmacy, biopharmaceutics and pharmacokinetics.

Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Medicinal, organic and inorganic chemistry, with structure–activity relationships.

Pharmacology

Drug action across every body system, toxicology and bioassays.

Pharmacognosy

Crude drugs, phytochemistry and herbal drug technology.

Pharmaceutical Analysis

Instrumental methods, titrimetric analysis and quality control.

Biochemistry & Microbiology

Metabolism, enzymes, microbiology and pharmaceutical biotechnology.

Clinical Pharmacy

Pharmacotherapeutics, hospital and community pharmacy practice.

Anatomy & Physiology

Human body systems, physiology and pathophysiology.

Also covered: Pharmaceutical Jurisprudence, Pharmaceutical Engineering and Biostatistics — every topic mapped into the weekly test cycle.
