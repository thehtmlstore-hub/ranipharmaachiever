How It Works

One focused 7-month GPAT program in three phases — with a weekly test, a results report and a mentor watching your progress the whole way.

Your journey, step by step

1

Attend a free demo class

Join one free live class with the actual batch and the faculty who will teach you — not a sales demo. If it feels right, you join the program.

2

Build the foundations — months 1–3

Pharmaceutics, Pharmacology and Pharmaceutical Chemistry taught live at 5:00 AM or in the evening. Every session is recorded and available the same day.

3

Test yourself every week

A fresh test goes live every Monday in the real exam pattern. Attempt it online under timed conditions — results within 24 hours.

4

Get your feedback report

Your mentor reviews every test and shares your strong areas, weak topics and next-week focus — with you and your parents.

5

Go advanced with PYQs — months 4–6

The full GPAT syllabus to exam depth, 10 years of previous-year questions analysed topic by topic, and dedicated doubt-clearing slots.

6

Finish with the mock test sprint — month 7

Weekly full-length mocks with All-India style rank lists, exam-day strategy workshops and one-on-one mentorship calls.

FAQs

Quick answers about classes, tests and portal access. Can't find yours? A faculty member is one message away.

Questions & answers

How do the online classes work?

All classes are live and interactive — you see the faculty, ask questions and get answers in real time. Sessions run at 5:00 AM and in the evening on weekdays, plus weekend batches, so college-going students never miss a class. Every session is recorded and available the same day.

How do I get my Register Number and password?

Your Register Number (e.g., RPA24PH1042) and password are created by your faculty when you join the program, and shared with you directly.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 82200 28504. Your faculty will reset it after verifying your identity.

When are weekly test results published?

Your score appears the moment you submit, along with every wrong answer and its correct option. The evaluated results and your topic-wise feedback report follow within 24 hours — shared with you and your parents.

How can parents track progress?

Every week, parents receive the test score and a topic-wise progress report on their phone. Every month there is a scheduled video call with the mentor, and parents are welcome to sit in on any live class.

What is the medium of instruction?

Classes are conducted in English with Tamil explanations where needed, so students from Tamil-medium backgrounds are not left behind. Study material is in English, matching the actual exam pattern.

Can I attend a free demo class?

Yes — attend one free live class with the actual batch and the faculty member who will teach you. Not a rehearsed sales demo, a real class. Book it through the Contact page or by calling us.

Why are the Admin and Coordinator sign-ins locked?

Those portals open only after verification by the Rani Pharma Achievers team. Contact us if you need coordinator or admin access.
